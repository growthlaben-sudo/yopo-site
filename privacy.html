1. What we collect

  • WhatsApp conversations. Your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of messages you exchange with us (text, images, documents, voice notes).
  • Business contact details you choose to send us — company name, price lists, quotations, delivery terms.
  • Email correspondence sent to our address.
  • Website: this site is static and does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and continue an existing conversation.
  • To handle procurement: request price lists, agree on terms, place and track orders.
  • To keep a record of agreements with suppliers, candidates, and partners.
  • To meet accounting and tax obligations under Indonesian law.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising profiling. Messages initiated by us are sent only to people and companies that have a business relationship with us or have contacted us first.

3. Who can see it

Access is limited to authorised YOPO staff. Data is processed with the help of service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Meta Platforms — delivery of WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Google — our company email.
  • Our own server infrastructure, used to store conversation history securely.

We disclose data to authorities only where required by law.

4. How long we keep it

Conversation history is kept while the business relationship lasts and for up to 5 years afterwards, in line with Indonesian record-keeping requirements. Messages that carry no business value are deleted earlier.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to stop messaging you. Write to yopofrozenbar@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”, or simply reply STOP in the WhatsApp chat — we will stop writing to you.

6. Data deletion

To request deletion of the data we hold about you, email yopofrozenbar@gmail.com from the address you contacted us with, or send a message from the phone number used in the chat. We confirm and complete deletion requests within 30 days, except where records must be retained by law.

7. Security

Traffic to our systems is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to conversation history requires individual accounts, and access rights are removed when a staff member leaves.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
